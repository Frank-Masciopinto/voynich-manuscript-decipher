# h52: menstrual-blood → plant / moon-period / end-yoga reading (pre-registration)

Written and committed **before any h52 statistic was computed**. No translation claims.
Do not edit this file after it is committed.

## Question

Does Beinecke MS 408 behave as if its content were the following story (a thematic reading, not a decipherment)?

1. Women create living things without male sperm.
2. Menstrual blood put into water flowers into plants.
3. Each moon period yields a different plant.
4. The end of the book is a yoga-like / body-practice science.

We cannot read EVA words as English. The tests below ask whether the **section order, vocabulary links, zodiac cycling, published cribs, and end-section register** match that story better than chance nulls already used in this repository. A pass would be structural support for the *theme*; it would still not be a translation.

## Data

- Transcription: `data/transcriptions/ZL3b-n.txt` via `h09_lib.parse()` (paragraph `P` and label `L` loci).
- Quire / section / hand / bifolium map: `data/quires.csv` via `msdecipher.quires.load_quires`.
- Visual tags (pre-registered in h09, eye-tagged without reading text): `hypotheses/h09_image_context_cribs/data/visual_tags.csv` — nymphs, pools, green/blue water, roots, flowers, stars.
- Same-plant herbal–pharma pairs: `h09_lib.same_plant_pairs()`.
- Published crib fragments reused only as already published:
  - h01 sheet: `dy` claimed "water", `qo` claimed "memory seed" (`hypotheses/h01_tone_sheet/table.csv`).
  - h09 candidate word lists (global enrichment only; known to fail within-quire): top water/nymph/root/star words from `hypotheses/h09_image_context_cribs/results/candidate_word_meanings.csv`.
- Zodiac pages: section = `zodiac` (12 pages; Roman-script month names at circle centres are **not** in ZL3b — use page order within the zodiac block as the month sequence, as in h05/h06).
- End block: quire 20 `stars` pages `f103r`–`f116r` (the manuscript's last continuous text block). Held-out earlier comparison: quire 8 `stars` pages, and a length-matched early herbal block (first N tokens of herbal with the same hand×language mix when possible).

Numpy seed **52**. Permutations: **2,000** unless noted. Holm within each test family.

## Section roles (fixed before computing)

| Role | Sections / blocks |
|---|---|
| `plants` | `herbal`, `pharmaceutical` |
| `water_blood` | `biological` (bath/nymph pages); also pages with `nymphs+pools+green_water+blue_water > 0` |
| `moon` | `zodiac` (primary); `astronomical` secondary sensitivity |
| `end_yoga` | quire 20 `stars` |
| `other` | `cosmological`, early `stars` (Q8), `text-only` |

Hypothesized **narrative order** of roles (blood-in-water → moon → plants as product → end science):

`water_blood` < `moon` < `plants` < `end_yoga`

(Alternative order A2, also pre-registered: `moon` < `water_blood` < `plants` < `end_yoga`.)

Observed order of a role = mean folio index among pages in that role (folios numbered in `quires.csv` row order).

## Tests

### T1 — Structure fit (section order)

- Statistic: Spearman ρ between hypothesized role ranks (1..4) and observed mean folio ranks of those four roles.
- Also report Kendall τ of the four means against the hypothesized order.
- **Null:** 2,000 random permutations of the four role labels onto the four observed mean ranks (equivalently: shuffle hypothesized ranks).
- **P1 passes** if primary order has one-sided p < 0.05 (ρ larger than null) **and** ρ > 0.5. A2 is sensitivity only.

Expectation stated in advance: **P1 fails.** Most plant pages precede the baths; the zodiac sits before the biological section.

### T2 — Bath–plant vocabulary link

- For each hand that has both biological and herbal (or pharma) text, compute Jensen–Shannon divergence (base 2) between the biological bag and the herbal bag, and the Jaccard of the top-50 word types.
- **Shared-stem rate:** fraction of biological label words (`Ln` nymph / `Lt` tube) that have a unit-edit-distance ≤ 1 or onset-stripped 3-unit stem hit in herbal paragraph text of the **same hand**.
- **Null:** 2,000 permutations of folio bags **within hand × Currier language**, keeping bag sizes; also a section-label shuffle among non-zodiac pages within hand.
- Re-check h09 water/nymph candidate words: count how many remain enriched on biological pages after within-quire permutation (should match h09: ~0 after Holm).
- **P2 passes** if the bath↔plant stem-hit rate beats the within-HL null at Holm p < 0.05 **and** JS(biological, herbal) is **smaller** than JS(biological, stars) and JS(biological, zodiac) under the same null (baths closer to plants than to moon/end).

Expectation: **P2 fails** (h09/h10 already found bath enrichment is a quire/section effect).

### T3 — Moon–plant mapping

- On the 12 zodiac pages in page order, take each page's label vocabulary (and, separately, paragraph vocabulary if present).
- Score similarity of zodiac page *k*'s labels to (a) the herbal folio whose plant-ID sources most often place it in calendar slot *k* — **not used** (no reliable calendar↔plant table); instead (b) nearest herbal folios in absolute page distance, and (c) a cycling null.
- Primary statistic: autocorrelation of consecutive zodiac pages' label bigram cosine (already studied in h06) **plus** mean cosine of each zodiac page's labels to the global herbal vocabulary, tested against 2,000 random pairings of zodiac pages with random herbal folios (within-hand when both have hand labels).
- Secondary: do zodiac labels share stems with herbal labels more than with pharmaceutical jars / star labels? (type-stratified, within-quire null where possible).
- **P3 passes** if zodiac↔herbal stem overlap beats zodiac↔stars and zodiac↔pharma at Holm p < 0.05, **and** the 12-page sequence shows plant-similarity cycling above a shuffled-month null (circular shift of the herbal partners).

Expectation: **P3 fails** (h05/h06: no calendar structure beyond drift; h17/h18 plant cribs failed).

### T4 — "No male" vs female/water cribs (published only)

Do **not** invent EVA→English maps. Use only:

1. h01 published pairs: `dy`→water/biological (female/water side), `qo`→seed/herbal (seed/male-plant side).
2. h09 candidate lists tagged `water` / `nymph` (female/water side) vs `root` (plant/seed side). Star list is a control topic.

For each side, take the top-5 candidate words from h09's global list (already computed in h09; we do not re-select on MS for this test) and measure their log rate ratio on biological vs herbal pages, with 2,000 within-hand page permutations.

- **P4a (female/water stronger):** water+nymph words are more enriched on biological pages than root words are on herbal pages (difference of mean log-rate-ratios > 0, p < 0.05).
- **P4b (seed/male absent):** `qo` is *not* enriched on herbal/pharma relative to biological (one-sided); consistent with "no male seed" only if water side passes and seed side is flat or depleted.
- **P4 passes** only if P4a and P4b both pass **and** the water/nymph enrichment survives within-quire Holm (i.e. is not the h09 quire artefact).

Expectation: **P4 fails.** h01 already found `dy` hit is non-specific and `qo` is depleted in herbal; h09 within-quire Holm = 1.0 for water/nymph.

### T5 — End-of-book "yoga / instructional" register

Compare quire-20 stars (`end_yoga`) to (i) quire-8 stars and (ii) a length-matched early herbal sample:

- Line-length CV, paragraph-first gallows rate, positional MI (as in h30), type/token, new-type rate vs a rolling window, and held-out word bigram gain D1 (as in h19/h49; expect negative if like the rest of MS).
- **P5 passes** if end_yoga differs from Q8 stars on ≥ 3 of {gallows rate, positional MI, TTR, D1} at Holm p < 0.05 under 2,000 within-stars folio permutations **and** D1 on end_yoga is greater than D1 on early herbal by ≥ 0.05 bits (instructional/list-like direction, cf. Cucina in h30/h50).

Expectation: **P5 fails.** Q20 is another stars block in hand 3B; h30/h47/h50 found the same line/copy regime everywhere.

### T6 — Blood-in-water → later plant (directional)

On bifolia / quires that contain both a water-tagged page (biological, or visual water score > 0) and a plant page (herbal or pharma):

- Let W be the water-page vocabulary and P the plant-page vocabulary.
- Forward score: stem-hit rate of W tokens into P when the water page precedes the plant page in binding order.
- Reverse score: same when plant precedes water.
- **Null:** 2,000 shuffles of page order within the bifolium (or within quire if bifolium has < 2 informative pairs).
- **P6 passes** if forward − reverse > 0 at p < 0.05 and the absolute forward rate beats a within-hand random plant-page null.

Expectation: **P6 fails.** Most herbal pages precede Q13; few bifolia mix biological with herbal.

## Verdict rule

- **Match:** ≥ 4 of P1–P6 pass.
- **Partial nearby:** 2–3 pass, or only T1/T5 structural hints without vocabulary links.
- **No match:** ≤ 1 pass.

Also report which nearby readings fit better if this fails: baths as spa/balneology (section isolation), zodiac as calendar (h05 Roman months), pharma as jars (h09), end stars as recipe-like copy text (h30/h47) without yoga-specific register.

## Out of scope

- Inventing EVA glosses for blood, sperm, moon, yoga, chakra, etc.
- Claiming any word means menstrual blood.
- Image-only storytelling without a failed or passed text statistic.
